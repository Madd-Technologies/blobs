Add more with server plugins.

Optional Hermes plugins that run on your server and light up parts of the app. In the app, Settings → Server plugins shows which ones your server has and gives you the install command.

hermes-paperless

Paperless

Your Paperless-ngx documents, streamed straight into the app's viewer. Any bot can search and read them with the skill that comes with it, and the documents it mentions open with a tap.

hermes-drive

Google Drive

Files from Drive open in the app's viewer; Google Docs, Sheets and Slides open in Google's own apps. Any bot can find files with its Drive skill. Read only.

hermes-dashboard

Dashboards

Each bot keeps panels for you: lists, boards, numbers and charts, kept fresh by a script where it can be, with drafts to approve and buttons that hand work back.

hermes-bot-mobile

Push and live rooms

Notifications when a bot needs you, encrypted end to end to your phone through UnifiedPush, and live "who's writing" in rooms.

All MIT, in blobs-hermes-bot-plugins.

Your server, nothing in between.

Blobs talks straight to your own Hermes Agent, the same hermes dashboard that serves Hermes's web dashboard. There's no Blobs server, no account with us and no analytics.

Blobs
hermes
dashboardYour server
Your bots

  1. On the machine that runs Hermes, start the dashboard on an address your phone can reach. A Tailscale address keeps it private to your own devices. The first time, Hermes asks you to choose a username and password.

    hermes dashboard --no-open --host 100.x.y.z --port 9119

  2. Open Blobs, enter http://100.x.y.z:9119 and sign in. Your phone keeps the session in its keychain, never the password.

Tested with Hermes Agent 0.21.5 and 0.21.6. Taking over a screen needs a Linux host with Bot Screen. Push notifications on Android, dashboards, Paperless and Google Drive come from optional server plugins.
