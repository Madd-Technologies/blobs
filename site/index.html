Your agents, in your pocket.

Blobs is a mobile app for Hermes Agent. Watch your bots work, answer them when they need you, and take over their screen when it's quicker to do it yourself.

Join the beta Source on GitHub

Built for the moments a bot needs you

Hermes bots work on their own for hours. Blobs keeps the quiet parts quiet and brings you in only when it's your turn.

What it does

  • Your botsEvery bot on your server, with its face, what it's doing and its last word.
  • Live tool activityCommands, file reads and output, step by step while a turn runs.
  • Approvals and questionsAllow a command, choose an option or reply, without opening a laptop.
  • Needs youOne inbox for everything waiting on you, across every bot.
  • Take over the screenWatch a bot's desktop live, and take the keyboard and mouse when it's stuck.
  • RoomsPut several bots in one conversation and @mention who should answer.
  • Routines and dashboardsThe jobs each bot runs on a schedule, and panels it keeps up to date for you.
  • Files, voice and morePhotos, PDFs and voice memos in; math, diagrams and documents out; replies read aloud.

Your server, nothing in between

Blobs talks straight to your own Hermes Agent, the same hermes dashboard that serves Hermes's web dashboard. There's no Blobs server, no account with us and no analytics.

  1. On the machine that runs Hermes, start the dashboard on an address your phone can reach. A Tailscale address keeps it private to your own devices. The first time, Hermes asks you to choose a username and password.

    hermes dashboard --no-open --host 100.x.y.z --port 9119

  2. Open Blobs, enter http://100.x.y.z:9119 and sign in. Your phone keeps the session in its keychain, never the password.

Tested with Hermes Agent 0.21.5 and 0.21.6. Taking over a screen needs a Linux host with Bot Screen. Push notifications on Android, dashboards, Paperless and Google Drive come from optional server plugins.
